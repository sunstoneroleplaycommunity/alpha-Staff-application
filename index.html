Currently in Development

We are building Sunstone the right way.

Sunstone RolePlay™ is not rushing to open before the community is ready. Our current focus is building department structure, staff policies, member expectations, application systems, server standards, and a clean foundation for future patrols.

Because of this, staff applications are selective. We are looking for people with previous roleplay or moderation experience, strong communication skills, patience, maturity, and the ability to represent Sunstone professionally.

What We Are Looking For

Experienced. Mature. Professional.

Experienced Staff

Applicants should understand roleplay communities, moderation, chain of command, and how to handle issues calmly.

Mature Leadership

We need staff who can communicate respectfully, make fair decisions, and stay professional during difficult situations.

Development Mindset

Sunstone is still being built, so applicants must be willing to help create systems, improve ideas, and support growth.

Console Focused

Our community is designed for PlayStation 5 and Xbox players who want serious roleplay without needing a PC server.
